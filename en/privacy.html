Privacy Policy

For the Android app "Kidrobe" and this website · Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Deutsche Fassung (the German version is legally binding)

In short
  • Kidrobe has no internet permission and sends no data to us or anyone else.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • All photos and entries stay on your device.
  • If you buy the full version, Google Play processes your payment; we only receive order data from Google.

2. Data in the app

Kidrobe stores boxes, locations, items, photos and settings only locally inside the app on your device. We have no access to them. The app has no internet permission and therefore cannot connect to any server itself.

Since we receive no data from the app, we cannot view, disclose or delete it. You can delete it yourself at any time – in the app or by uninstalling it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera – to take photos of clothing. Photos are stored only inside the app.
  • Storage (Android 12 and lower only) – to read photos from the gallery and to read or save backup files.
  • Vibration – for short haptic feedback.
  • Google Play Billing – to handle the purchase of the full version through the Google Play app (see section 6).

Kidrobe does not request internet, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

4. Photo analysis on your device

The category suggestion (beta) and the color-based gender suggestion analyze the photo with a model bundled with the app or a simple color analysis. Both run entirely on your device; the photo is not transferred and not used for any other purpose.

5. Backup, sharing and printing

You can create a backup (ZIP file), an HTML overview with photos, or box labels (PDF). You pass these files on yourself via Android's share menu or print service – e.g. to Google Drive, a messenger, a cloud service or a printer. The respective provider is responsible for processing there, and their privacy policy applies. We do not receive these files.

Android's automatic backup (Auto Backup to your Google account) is turned off for Kidrobe so your data is never stored in a cloud without your action. Uninstalling therefore deletes all data unless you exported a backup first.

6. Buying the full version via Google Play

The full version is a one-time purchase in Google Play. Payment, billing and payment data are processed exclusively by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) under its privacy policy. We do not receive payment details such as card numbers.

Through the Google Play Console, Google provides us with order data, e.g. order number, purchase date, product, price, country or region, and status (e.g. refunded). We use it to fulfil the contract, for refunds and support requests, and for our accounting.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract) and (c) (legal retention obligations).
  • Retention: as long as commercial and tax retention periods require.

So that a purchase also applies after reinstalling or on another device, Kidrobe asks the Google Play app on your device at startup whether a purchase exists. The result is stored only locally. This check runs through the Google Play app; Kidrobe itself does not connect to the internet.

7. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the message once the request is settled and no retention obligation applies.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, reliable delivery). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub bases such transfers on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

This website sets no cookies, uses no JavaScript, no analytics and loads no third-party content (the fonts are hosted on this website too). Links to Google Play lead to Google's pages, where Google's privacy policy applies.

9. Children

Kidrobe is intended for parents and other adults who manage children's clothing. We do not collect data from children.

10. Your rights

Where we process personal data about you (order data, emails), you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Just send us an email.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. in your country of residence or the authority responsible for us.

11. Changes

If the app or this website changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
