Help & FAQ

Data & privacy

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Kidrobe has no internet permission, no account and no server. Photos, boxes and items only leave your device when you share a backup or an overview yourself.

Are my photos uploaded or analyzed?

No. The category suggestion and the color suggestion run entirely on your device. Nothing is transferred.

What happens when I uninstall?

All data is deleted. Kidrobe deliberately does not use Android's automatic Google backup, so your data never ends up in a cloud without you noticing. Export a backup first (see below).

Backup & new phone

How do I move to a new phone?
  • Old phone: Menu › Backup › Export Backup. Save or share the ZIP file (e.g. Google Drive, Nextcloud, USB cable).
  • New phone: install Kidrobe, Menu › Backup › Import Backup and pick the file.

Existing data is merged, not overwritten – newer entries take priority.

Can I use two devices together?

There is no automatic sync (that would need internet access). But you can import a backup from one device on the other – changes are merged.

How do I show grandma my boxes?

Menu › Backup › Share Overview creates an HTML file with photos. It opens in the browser on any phone or computer – no app needed.

Full version & purchase

What is free?

Your first 20 items. Boxes and locations are unlimited. The full version unlocks unlimited items – pay once, no subscription.

I already bought it – how do I get the full version on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same account. Usually the purchase is recognized automatically at startup. If not: Settings › Unlock full version › Restore purchase › Check with Google Play. If you already bought it, you won't be charged.

Does the purchase cover my family?

Yes, the full version is enabled for Google Play Family Library.

How does the purchase work without internet permission?

The purchase is handled by the Google Play app on your device. Kidrobe itself never connects to the internet.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play (play.google.com › Order history). You can also email me.

Labels & QR codes

Which labels do I need?

Universal labels 70 × 42 mm, 21 per A4 sheet. In Kidrobe: box view › Print box labels, select boxes, print or save as PDF.

How do I open a box via QR code?

Just scan it with your regular phone camera – the box opens right in Kidrobe. Without a label: Find box by code and enter the 3-digit code.

Other

Is Kidrobe available for iPhone?

Currently Android only. The shared HTML overview opens on an iPhone too, though.
