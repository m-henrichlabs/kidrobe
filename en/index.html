Kidrobe makes your clothing boxes searchable. Photograph each item once, add size and season – and find it again in seconds. For younger siblings, the next season or selling later.

Get it on Google Play

For Android. English and German.

Locations & boxes

Attic, basement, kids' room – every box shows a photo collage of what's inside and gets a 3-digit code.

The right sizes

Clothing, shoe, sock and hat sizes depending on the category, plus season, condition and "runs large/small".

Found in seconds

Search by title or note, filter by category, size and season – and see right away which box it's in.

QR labels

Print labels as a PDF (70 × 42 mm, 21 per A4 sheet). Scan the QR code with your phone camera – the box opens in Kidrobe.

Category suggestion

Kidrobe suggests a category from the photo (beta) – entirely on your device, nothing is uploaded.

Share & back up

An HTML overview with photos for the grandparents, a backup file for a new phone – you decide where it goes.
